## Problem

I started thinking about Noise after noticing how difficult it has become to focus online without being interrupted. Notifications, short videos, unread messages, pop-ups, ads and endless scrolling are always competing for attention, and over time that constant stimulation can feel mentally draining.

A lot of digital platforms are designed to keep people engaged for as long as possible. The experience can feel fast and entertaining in the moment, but it can also leave you distracted, overstimulated and tired without fully realising why.

With Noise, I wanted to turn that feeling into an editorial experience. Instead of designing an app, I used typography, repetition, contrast, crowded layouts and pacing to show what digital interruption feels like on the page.

## Visual direction

For Noise, I wanted the editorial to feel like digital overstimulation on paper. I wanted it to move between quiet reading moments and louder interruptions, the same way a screen can pull your attention back and forth without you even noticing.

I used bold type, repetition, layered text, strong contrast and crowded layouts to show what it feels like to be constantly interrupted online. Some pages are meant to feel tense or overwhelming, but I still used hierarchy, spacing and pacing so the reader could follow the piece.

Archivo Black is used for the bold, urgent text, while Bodoni Moda is used for the slower reading sections. Together with the high-contrast colours, I wanted the publication to feel loud, repetitive and anxious, but still clear enough to read and move through.

**Type:** Archivo Black and Bodoni Moda.
