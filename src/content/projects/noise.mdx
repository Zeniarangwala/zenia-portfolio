## The problem

I started thinking about Noise after noticing how hard it's become to focus online without being interrupted. Notifications, short videos, unread messages, pop-ups, ads and endless scrolling are always competing for attention, and over time that's draining.

A lot of platforms are designed to keep people engaged as long as possible. It feels fast and fun in the moment, but it can leave you distracted, overstimulated and tired without really knowing why.

So I wanted to turn that feeling into an editorial. Instead of designing an app, I used typography, repetition, contrast, crowded layouts and pacing to show what digital interruption feels like on the page.

## Visual direction

I wanted the editorial to feel like digital overstimulation on paper. It moves between quiet reading moments and louder interruptions, the same way a screen pulls your attention back and forth without you noticing.

Bold type, repetition, layered text, strong contrast and crowded layouts show what it feels like to be constantly interrupted. Some pages are meant to feel tense, but I still used hierarchy, spacing and pacing so you can follow the piece.

- **Type:** Archivo Black for the bold, urgent text. Bodoni Moda for the slower reading sections.
- **Colour:** White #FFFFFF, Ink #0A0A0A, Red #FF3B16, Lime #D4FF3A, Indigo #1C1066, Magenta #FF2BD6, Amber #FFB700 and Grey #8B8B8B.

Together, I wanted it to feel loud, repetitive and anxious, but still clear enough to read.
