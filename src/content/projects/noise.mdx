## Problem

Noise started when I noticed I couldn't read anything online without being interrupted. Notifications, short videos, unread messages, pop-ups and ads all compete for the same attention, and after a while it's draining.

Most apps are built to keep you on them as long as possible. In the moment it's fun, but afterwards you're tired and not sure why.

I didn't want to design another app about it. I wanted to make the feeling itself, in print, with type, repetition, contrast, crowded layouts and pacing.

## Visual direction

The editorial switches between quiet reading and loud interruption, the way a screen pulls you back and forth without you noticing.

Two typefaces do that job. Archivo Black is the interruption: heavy, urgent, repeated. Bodoni Moda is the slow reading voice. Some pages are meant to be uncomfortable, but hierarchy and spacing still tell you where to look first, so you can always find your way through.
