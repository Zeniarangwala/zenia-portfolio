KidCash is a mobile app that helps kids learn how to earn, save and spend money through fun, interactive tasks. With a digital allowance system, kids complete chores to earn virtual cash they can spend, save or donate, all while learning real-world money habits.

## Why I created KidCash

I've always wished I had learned how to manage money earlier. Growing up, I spent without thinking and saved without really understanding why, mostly because no one ever taught me how money actually works.

That's what inspired KidCash. I wanted to design a financial learning app that feels fun and engaging without being overly childish or boring. The goal was to help kids build healthier money habits early, through interactive tasks, rewards and simple financial goals they can actually connect with.

## Understanding the problem

Research that shaped the direction of KidCash:

- Children begin developing attitudes and emotional behaviours around money from a very young age, shaping how they approach spending and saving later in life.
- Children can develop very different relationships with money early on, which highlights the importance of guidance, structure and real-life financial learning experiences.
- As digital spending becomes more common, money can start to feel less tangible for kids, changing how they understand spending habits and financial responsibility.
- A TD survey found that 86% of Canadian parents agreed that interactive games that teach finances could make learning about money more fun for kids. This reinforced my decision to make KidCash more interactive, reward-based and visually engaging.

Sources: TD Stories, “Your Kid's Digital Spending Habits”; Michigan Ross, “New Research Shows Children Form Attitudes About Money at a Young Age”; Benjamin Talks, “Spendthrift or Penny Pincher? The Psychology Behind Your Kid and Money”.
