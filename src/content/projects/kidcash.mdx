## Why I made KidCash

I've always wished I'd learned how to manage money earlier. Growing up, I spent without thinking and saved without really understanding why, mostly because nobody ever taught me how money works.

So I designed KidCash, a mobile app that helps kids learn to earn, save and spend through fun, interactive tasks. Kids do chores to earn virtual cash they can spend, save or donate. I wanted it to feel fun without being too childish or boring.

## What the research said

- Kids start forming attitudes and emotions around money really young, and that shapes how they spend and save later.
- Kids can develop very different relationships with money early on, so guidance, structure and real-life learning matter.
- As spending goes digital, money can feel less real to kids.
- A TD survey found 86% of Canadian parents agreed that interactive games that teach finances could make learning about money more fun for kids. That pushed me to make KidCash more interactive, reward-based and visual.

Sources: TD Stories, “Your Kid's Digital Spending Habits”; Michigan Ross, “New Research Shows Children Form Attitudes About Money at a Young Age”; Benjamin Talks, “Spendthrift or Penny Pincher? The Psychology Behind Your Kid and Money”.

### What I drew from

I looked at gamified banking and savings app designs on Pinterest, and at three apps:

- **Greenlight:** the spend / save / give system.
- **GoHenry:** chore rewards and earning.
- **Duolingo:** a friendly mascot and progress loops.

### Look and feel

- **Colour:** teal #2A7F8E, yellow #F4C660, pink #F4A2A2, white #FFFFFF, cream #FBEFD9 and dark navy #1B2A33.
- **Type:** Nunito as the primary font.
