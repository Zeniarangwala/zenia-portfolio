## Why I created KidCash

I've always wished I had learned how to manage money earlier. Growing up, I spent without thinking and saved without really understanding why, mostly because no one ever taught me how money actually works.

KidCash is the app I wish I'd had. Kids do chores to earn a digital allowance, then decide what to spend, save or give. I wanted it to be fun without being childish or boring.

## Understanding the problem

What I found:

- Kids start forming attitudes about money very young, and those habits stick.
- When money is mostly taps and cards, it stops feeling real.
- A TD survey found 86% of Canadian parents think games that teach money would make it more fun for kids.

So saving had to be something you can see (a tree that grows, bars that fill up), and learning had to be a game.

Sources: TD Stories, “Your Kid's Digital Spending Habits”; Michigan Ross, “New Research Shows Children Form Attitudes About Money at a Young Age”; Benjamin Talks, “Spendthrift or Penny Pincher? The Psychology Behind Your Kid and Money”.
